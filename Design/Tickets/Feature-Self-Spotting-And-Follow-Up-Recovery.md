# Self-Spotting & Follow-Up-Shot Recovery

Status: open
Filed: 2026-08-13

Cartridge-scaled recoil already makes the sight-picture loss accurate — a .22 barely moves, a .50 loses
the target entirely — but nothing is built on top of it. The mechanics that matter are calling your own
impact from what you saw before the rifle jumped, and getting back on target for a follow-up shot.
