# Range C — ELR Ladder To 2000

Status: open
Filed: 2026-08-13

A 250-step ladder to 2000 on a convex rising slope, where the scope's elevation-travel ceiling is the
designed lesson and holdover rather than a gear gate is the answer (2000 m needs 23.02 MIL against a
20 MIL flat base). De-risked on device by the 2026-07-28 probe: an 18 s time of flight is fine, the iPad
holds 60 fps at 3 km, the depth buffer is 24-bit, and trace, impact and ping all read at 2000 m. The
plan document still needs a rewrite against those findings before build starts.
