# Spotter Cam

Status: open
Filed: 2026-08-13

A small secondary view fed by a camera set a few yards off the committed target and pointed at it, so the
player can see where on the face a round landed. The motivating fact is angular: a splat on a 1 MIL gong
at 1500–2000 m is a few tenths of a mrad across, well under the scope's own resolution at usable
magnification, so the far half of an ELR range otherwise gives hit/miss and nothing else — not enough to
true a curve against. Requires a committed target (owner, 2026-07-27), which is the point rather than a
limitation; open questions on always-on vs toggled, live vs freeze-frame, and iPad cost are in
`Design/feature-catalog.md` §D.
