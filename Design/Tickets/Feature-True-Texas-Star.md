# True Texas Star

Status: open
Filed: 2026-08-13

The real Texas star: plates that DROP out of a cradle when hit and unbalance the rotating wheel, so
every hit changes the timing of the next shot. The shipped popper star is the deliberately simpler
fold-and-latch version the owner asked for; this is logged as the fidelity gap that leaves behind, and it
depends on `Feature-Dropping-Plate-Targets`.
