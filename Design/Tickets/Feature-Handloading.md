# Handloading

Status: open
Filed: 2026-08-13

Author a load — custom bullet shape and core plus powder charge — tuned to one specific rifle for low SD.
It must be developed rather than bought: vary the charge, chronograph, find the node. Per-rifle,
reduces vertical dispersion only (the wind call is untouched), so it is end-game ELR optimisation rather
than a default win button.
