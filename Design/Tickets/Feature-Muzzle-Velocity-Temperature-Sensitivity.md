# Temperature Sensitivity of Muzzle Velocity

Status: open
Filed: 2026-08-13

A per-load temperature-sensitivity characteristic — temp-stable versus temp-sensitive powders — so a DOPE
card trued on a warm day drifts on a cold one. Distinct from air density's effect on drag, which is
already modelled; this feeds the hidden-truth ammo model and interacts with weather conditions.
