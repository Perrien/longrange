# Mirage As A Readable Wind-Call Instrument

Status: open
Filed: 2026-08-13

The layered heat mirage renders faithfully but reads as atmosphere rather than information — a real
shooter calls wind speed and direction off boil and drift. Wiring it to a genuine read (a HUD hook off
the same smoothed wind vector the bullet flies through) was deferred from the wind port as a separate
design question.
